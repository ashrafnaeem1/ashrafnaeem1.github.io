🍉 Fun Facts

Watermelons are technically a type of berry, or more specifically, a pepo. A pepo is a type of berry (from the botanical definition) that has a hard, thick rind and is the characteristic fruit of the gourd family, Cucurbitaceae, which also includes cucumbers and squashes!

The heaviest watermelon ever recorded weighed 350.5 pounds (159 kilograms), which is heavier than a refrigerator! It was grown by Chris Kent of Sevierville, Tennessee, USA in 2013, earning a place in the Guinness World Records.

Watermelon is a symbol of Peaceful resistance against apartheid, colonisation and illegal occupation. It is a symbol that has long demonstrated that, despite all odds, strong willed peaceful people will peacefully continue working for what's right.
